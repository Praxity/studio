# Praxity Studio licensing

Praxity Studio is source-available under the unmodified
[PolyForm Perimeter License 1.0.1](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/perimeter/1.0.1).
The `LICENSE` file is authoritative. This page explains it in everyday language.

These terms cover Praxity Studio as released: the desktop app and the
`praxity` command-line tool. This repository's research, internal
documentation and unreleased code are not published and not licensed.

## Free to use

Use and change Praxity Studio without charge, at home and at work. That includes
personal projects, teaching and research, nonprofit and government work,
internal use at any company, and paid client work where Praxity Studio is one of the
tools you use to do the job.

## What the licence rules out

You may not use Praxity Studio to provide others with a product that competes with it.
The licence's test is whether a product, which can be a good or a service, is
marketed as a substitute for what Praxity Studio does. Selling, hosting, repackaging or
white-labelling Praxity Studio falls in that category, and so does a service sold
mainly as Praxity Studio's function.

If your business adds its own work on top, such as course design, content or
training, you don't need to ask. If it mainly resells what Praxity Studio does, talk to
me first. I'm glad to work together when improvements and test results flow
back into Praxity Studio.

Want to embed Praxity Studio in your product or service, or run a free public fork or
service? Contact me at [hello@arielharlap.com](mailto:hello@arielharlap.com).
I usually say yes to free public projects.

## Earlier versions

Studio 0.2.0 shipped with Praxity software terms 1.0, the Ethical Use Policy
1.0 and a component schedule. Studio 0.1.8 and earlier shipped with the Studio
EULA. Copies of those versions keep those terms. The archived texts are in
`legal/archive/`.

## Courses you make

You own your course content. Every export includes
`PRAXITY-COURSE-RUNTIME-LICENSE.txt`, which lets you and your recipients host,
run and distribute the course, including commercially, without a Studio
licence.

## The .prax format

The specification in `docs/prax-format/` is CC BY 4.0, its examples are CC0
1.0, and its skill files and code are MIT. See that folder's `LICENSE`.
