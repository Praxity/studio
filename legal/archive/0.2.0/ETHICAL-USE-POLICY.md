# Praxity Ethical Use Policy

Version 1.0. Effective September 14, 2026. Adopted by the owner; no external legal review.

Praxity Studio is intended to support learning that respects human dignity,
privacy and autonomy, peace, ecological responsibility, fair work, shared
prosperity, community health, and community self-determination.

This policy forms part of the [Praxity software terms](EULA.md) for expressly covered Studio UI and modules, under free, trial and paid permissions. It includes use through APIs, CLIs, LLMs and third-party interfaces. Payment does not waive it. Separately licensed material remains governed by its own terms and is outside this policy's scope.
It applies to the purpose and content of training created, delivered, or
materially facilitated with covered Studio UI or modules. It does not prohibit an organization
from using Studio for a permitted training purpose solely because of unrelated
activities by that organization.

You may not use covered Studio UI or modules to create, deliver, or materially facilitate
training primarily intended to do any of the following.

## Surveillance and privacy

- Enable mass or indiscriminate surveillance, covert tracking, non-consensual
  biometric identification, or social scoring.
- Monitor workers, labour organizers, protesters, political activity,
  religious activity, or other protected association without a legitimate,
  proportionate, and rights-respecting purpose.
- Facilitate unlawful or deceptive collection, inference, sale, disclosure, or
  exploitation of personal or sensitive information.
- Circumvent informed consent, privacy safeguards, data-subject requests, or
  other applicable privacy rights.
- Teach deceptive interface practices intended to obtain or manufacture
  consent.

This section does not prohibit legitimate privacy or cybersecurity education,
proportionate security and fraud prevention, rights-respecting accessibility
analytics, or other uses supported by consent, necessity, safeguards, and due
process.

## Labour, economic exploitation, and community wealth

- Facilitate wage theft, forced labour, unlawful child labour, or worker
  misclassification intended to evade legal protections.
- Suppress lawful worker organizing, collective bargaining, or protected
  labour activity, including retaliation against participants.
- Facilitate predatory lending or debt collection, fraud, deceptive
  manipulation, or discriminatory denial of employment, housing, healthcare,
  credit, or other essential services.
- Dispossess Indigenous peoples or communities of land, resources, knowledge,
  or economic benefit without free, prior, and informed consent.
- Conceal or facilitate severe, foreseeable harm to a community's health,
  environment, essential resources, or economic security.

This section does not prohibit legitimate business education,
entrepreneurship, financial literacy, worker safety, labour-rights education,
collective bargaining, cooperatives, community economic development, or
just-transition work.

## Climate and environment

- Disseminate materially false or deceptive climate information intended to
  obstruct mitigation, adaptation, public understanding, or community
  resilience.
- Facilitate unlawful environmental destruction or knowingly conceal severe,
  widespread, or long-term environmental harm.

This section does not prohibit good-faith scientific discussion, media
literacy, climate adaptation and remediation, environmental compliance, or
just-transition education.

## Military activity, violence, and coercion

- Support weapons development or operation, targeting, combat operations,
  coercive interrogation, torture, unlawful detention, or other unlawful
  military activity.
- Incite violence, persecution, or discrimination against people or protected
  groups.

This section does not prohibit peacekeeping, humanitarian response, demining,
conflict prevention, civilian protection, or human-rights and law-of-armed-
conflict education.

## Questions and reports

Praxity will assess credible reports in context and provide notice and an
opportunity to cure as described in section 11 of the Praxity software terms, including at least 30 days to cure where reasonably practicable and the existing exception for unlawful use or credible risk of imminent and serious harm. Praxity does not inspect local
course files or add surveillance to police this policy.

Questions or reports may be sent to
